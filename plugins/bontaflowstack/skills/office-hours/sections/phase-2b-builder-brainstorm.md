# Builder brainstorming

For learning, personal tools and creative projects, identify the useful or
interesting outcome, time budget and audience. Reuse answered questions.

Explore only what helps: what would be satisfying to use, the nearest existing
tool, one distinctive idea and the quickest demonstrable version. A larger
vision can be noted without adding it to the current scope. Keep research-only
requests as research questions; do not turn them into a build plan.

Compare a minimal approach with a meaningful alternative when needed. Finish
with concrete next steps and an observable success condition. Do not demand
revenue, market validation, named customers or repeated approval for settled
choices. Any sketch or prototype is optional and follows the user's scope;
this skill itself produces the design brief.
